Transformer architectures have become the dominant paradigm in natural language processing. Self-attention allows models to capture long-range dependencies without recurrence. Nevertheless, discourse-level understanding remains an open challenge. Researchers have proposed hierarchical models that operate over elementary discourse units. For example, recent work combines RST-style parsing with neural encoders. Consequently, applications such as summarization and question answering have begun to benefit from explicit discourse structure. The field continues to explore how questions under discussion can guide parsing decisions.
